02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I’m pursuing a B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering, specializing in Artificial Intelligence & Robotics, at Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology.

I enjoy the space between an algorithm and something you can actually use. That has led me to robotic manipulators, computer vision applications, networking tools, Unreal Engine games, and small products that solve everyday problems.

My approach is hands-on: understand the problem, build a working version, inspect what fails, and improve it. I’m currently sharpening my C++, Python, and problem-solving skills while developing AURA, an indoor robotic assistant.
